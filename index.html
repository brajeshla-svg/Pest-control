Tips for an accurate result

  • Take a close-up in daylight, in focus.
  • Show the pest itself, or the damaged part (leaf, stem, fruit).
  • Include a healthy part of the plant next to the damage if you can.
  • One problem per photo works best.

AI can be wrong. Treat this as guidance only and confirm with your local agriculture officer (KVK) before spraying any chemical. Always follow the pesticide label.
